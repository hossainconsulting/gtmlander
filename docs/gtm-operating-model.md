## Proposed handoffs

1. **Audience and offer:** Define one trade, one Sydney market segment and a specific service offer. Record eligibility, exclusions and evidence for each claim.
2. **Content and capture:** Publish only approved material. Capture enquiries through a verified channel with a clear source and permission basis.
3. **Initial qualification:** Collect business type, service need, location, urgency, permission to respond and missing information. Draft a response for review.
4. **Human review:** Hemayet checks fit, accuracy, tone and any sensitive situation, then decides whether to contact or schedule.
5. **Discovery and proposal:** Hemayet leads the conversation, records the agreed need, reviews a tailored proposal and approves sending.
6. **Outcome and learning:** Record disposition and reason, then review which channels and messages produced qualified conversations.

**Approval gates:** No automatic cold calling, bulk messaging, social DMs, email sending, meeting booking, price commitments, contracts or payments are authorized by this diagram. Keep customer consent, channel rules and data retention under review before a pilot involving real people.

## First small pilot and evidence

Start with one synthetic Sydney plumbing-business journey. Write the audience definition, sample enquiry, qualification checklist, draft response, human decision, discovery questions and proposal outline. Link each transition to a future test case in the GTM Agents product backlog if functionality is built. For a real pilot, use only an approved source and record actual dates, counts and denominators.

Proposed measures: enquiries by source, permission status, response time, qualified conversations, meetings held, proposals reviewed/sent and won work. No conversion or revenue result is established yet. A product interface, an agency process and a Salesforce simulation are distinct artefacts; cross-link them rather than presenting one as the other.
